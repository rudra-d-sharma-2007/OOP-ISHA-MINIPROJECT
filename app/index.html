The rectangle is 100 × 60 units. Positive Tx moves it right; positive Ty moves it down in this screen view.

Original position Translated position
Enter values and select “Apply Translation”.

Formula Used

x′ = x + Tx
y′ = y + Ty

Here, (x, y) are the original coordinates, (Tx, Ty) are the translation distances, and (x′, y′) are the new coordinates.
